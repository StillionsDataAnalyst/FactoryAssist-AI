# Five-Minute Executive Demo

## 1. Establish the problem

“An operator sees pressure fluctuations on Hydraulic Press #4. Today, the operator may search a binder, call a supervisor, or wait for maintenance.”

## 2. Show operator workflow

1. Select `PRESS-004`.
2. Enter: `The press is losing pressure and cycling slowly.`
3. Select **Generate reaction plan**.

Emphasize:

- Structured, approved actions
- Safety restrictions
- Escalation path
- SOP reference

## 3. Show maintenance intelligence

Open the Maintenance view and explain:

- Previous similar failure
- Recommended first inspection point
- Machine-specific history

## 4. Show executive value

Open the Manager view and explain:

- Equipment health
- Repeat-issue visibility
- Reliability priorities
- Training opportunities

## 5. Close

“FactoryAssist AI does not replace technicians. It delivers approved plant knowledge at the moment an employee needs it.”
